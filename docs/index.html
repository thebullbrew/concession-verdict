How to use the concession like an adult

Ask for the concession as a straight rent cut and watch the room change. A free month is worth real money in year one and nothing in year two — the renewal is priced off the inflated face rent forever. A cut, by contrast, compounds in your favour at every renewal. If they will not cut, take the free months up front, bank the difference, and price your renewal decision off the face rent — never off the number you got used to paying.
